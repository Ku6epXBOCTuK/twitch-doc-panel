## Please do not

- Name future bosses **in public chat**
- Link walkthroughs of the current chapter
- Discuss the ending before the credits roll

## You absolutely may

- Hype up the next area vaguely
- Share fan theories — with a `#theory` tag
- Spoil away in the spoiler channel, we keep it mapped

> First offense gets a **10-minute timeout**. Repeat spills get banned during
> the campaign.
