## Global emotes

Available to everyone:

- `Pog` — hype moments
- `Poggers` — extra hype
- `KKona` — streamer champion

## Subscriber emotes

Unlock by subbing:

- `Bravo` — good play
- `Copium` — tough loss
- `Clap` — appreciated clip
- `Hmm` — big think

## Use them

Try `Bravo Bravo` in chat when the boss drops. You'll know when.
