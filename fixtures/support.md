## Ways to support the stream

1. **Subscribe** — cheapest way to help and unlocks emotes
2. **Bits** — cheer for clip-worthy plays
3. **Gift subs** — share the love with lurkers
4. **Watch** — the simplest one, comes free

## Donation policy

All tips go straight into:

- stream hardware upgrades
- new game keys
- weekly giveaways

> Tips are never required. The stream stays free either way.
