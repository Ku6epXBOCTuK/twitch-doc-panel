## Frequently asked questions

**When do you go live?** Check the schedule page — times can shift, so follow
the channel.

**Can I play with you?** Game nights are announced in chat a week ahead. Watch
for the signup message.

**Where is the replay?** VODs stay on the channel for 14 days, then move to
highlights.

## Quick answers

- **Subs** — know when extra games happen
- **Bits** — used for on-screen suggestions
- **Gift subs** — the countdown wheel gets a spin
