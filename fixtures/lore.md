## The story so far

The guild split after the fall of the Sunwatch Tower. The old captain went north
with half the maps; you inherited the **broken compass**.

## What we learned

1. The tower fell to a voice that everyone heard but nobody saw
2. The compass points not to treasure but to the _last living lighthouse_
3. The north maps are coded — mods found symbols in the stream art
